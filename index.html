Limitations & intended use
This tool provides screening-level estimates only. True heat stress (WBGT) requires field instruments measuring wind speed, solar radiation, and humidity — such as a Kestrel Heat Stress Meter. Modeled wet bulb temperature cannot account for radiant heat load or wind cooling, and may underestimate risk on sunny, calm days. This tool is not a substitute for validated on-site measurement in occupational, athletic, or clinical settings. Thresholds shown are for healthy adults at rest; risk is lower for elderly, children, and those with health conditions. Always follow local heat advisories.
